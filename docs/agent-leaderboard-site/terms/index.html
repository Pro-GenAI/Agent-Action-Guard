Classifier and benchmark scope

Classification can miss harmful actions and flag safe actions. Use the classifier alongside authorization, sandboxing, and appropriate human review. It does not guarantee that an agent, application, or deployment is safe or legally compliant.

Published accuracy and latency describe the reported dataset and test conditions. Local-system latency comparisons use CPU execution and exclude warm-up and model loading. Results can differ with hardware, dependency versions, embedding backends, thresholds, and workloads. Linked mentions or repository stars are not product reviews, proof of performance, or endorsements by the people or their employers.
